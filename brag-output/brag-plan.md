# Brag Plan: ALINE

## What is this app?
ALINE is a personal AI agent for the life you have outside work — it learns your real rhythms from behaviour (not forms), tells you what's actually happening near you, and rearranges your week so plans with people you like actually happen.

## The angle
Every shared-calendar app shows you when everyone is free. None of them make anything happen. ALINE's agent does the asking, the suggesting, and — the part nobody else does — the **moving**. The video's whole job is to land one idea: *a calendar reads your life; ALINE rearranges it.*

Specific to this project, not generic: the warm-paper palette pulled from the logo, the real chat UI with event cards, and a real rearrangement of a real week.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Warm paper. Nothing else. Two short lines settle, calm and confident:
**"Everyone's free."** then **"Nobody makes the plan."**
No music swell, no motion tricks — the restraint IS the hook, and it states the exact problem in six words.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The composer typing **"what's happening this weekend?"** character by character in the real UI — warm cream background, teal send arrow.
- The agent's reply is **one line** ("Two things on this weekend.") and then two real **event cards** slide in one by one — poster thumbnail, `Fri 20:30 - Kino Siska - 10 EUR - ~12 min drive`, and an `I'm going` pill. The restraint of the one-line reply is itself a product feature.
- **The move:** tapping `I'm going` surfaces a clash, and a flexible "Strength training" block slides from Friday to Saturday to open the slot. The jazz night drops into the gap.

## Outro / punchline
Cards clear. The ALINE mark settles on warm paper with one line: **"Your agent makes it happen."**

## User flow worth showing
Real three-beat flow, taken from the working app:
1. **Entry** — user asks the agent "what's happening this weekend?" in chat.
2. **Key action** — agent returns one line + structured event cards; user taps `I'm going`.
3. **Result** — the event lands on the week, and the movable block shifts aside to make room.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film — warm paper, calm confidence, the agent doing the work while the user barely lifts a finger
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, no flashing. The product's own philosophy is "calm, anti-addictive: no bright reds, no urgent colours, no gamification" — the video must obey the same rule or it contradicts the brand. Energy comes from crisp entrances and one decisive motion (the block sliding), not from cutting fast.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20s

## Share copy (draft)
Your calendar knows when you're free. ALINE knows what you'd actually want to do — and moves things around so it happens.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, low bed with restrained motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM) — warm and forward without being hype
- Music treatment: start at 0:00 under the hook at low volume, lift slightly as the UI appears (~3.7s), hold steady, fade out over the last 1.5s into the logo
- Music cue guidance: preset cue file read (`assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`). Target strong cues at **3.70s** (hook to UI), **8.44s** and **10.54s** (the two event cards arriving), **12.65s** (the `I'm going` tap). Beat grid is ~0.52s; card reveals sit ~2.1s apart, comfortably above the readability floor.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — allow the teal accent glow on the logo to breathe with music energy in the outro only. No waveform bars, no pulsing UI.
- SFX posture: sparse and motion-matched. Soft key ticks under the typed question, one quiet card arrival per event card, one confirming tap on `I'm going`, one soft settle as the schedule block lands.
- Audio-coupled moments: typed question (key ticks), two sequential card reveals (beat-aligned), the tap, the block slide.
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no risers, no notification chimes, nothing that sounds like an app demanding attention. If a cue feels "exciting", it is wrong for this brand.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The problem — 3.5s
Full-bleed warm paper `#F5F1EA`. Centered, in deep teal-charcoal display type: **"Everyone's free."** holds ~0.9s, then a second line settles beneath: **"Nobody makes the plan."** Both lines hold together to the cut. No UI yet, no logo yet. Wide margins, lots of empty paper.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two lines arrive one after the other, each fast-in (~0.35s) then held.
Audio intent: quiet, almost pre-roll. Music enters low and warm; the silence around the type does the work.
Audio-coupled idea: none — let the type land dry.
Music: warm low bed, entering.
Transition mood: soft to Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The ask — 3.5s
Cut to the app. Phone-shaped frame on warm paper, showing the real ALINE chat screen: the "ALINE" wordmark header with the teal `Events` and `Goals` pills, empty thread, composer at the bottom. The question types into the composer character by character: **"what's happening this weekend?"** The teal send arrow pulses once and the message posts as a user bubble.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated typing, then a simulated send tap.
Audio intent: the product waking up; music lifts very slightly on the cut.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle key ticks under the typing, one soft tap on send.
Music: same bed, small lift at the ~3.70s strong cue.
Transition mood: clean to Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The answer — 5s
The agent replies with a single short line: **"Two things on this weekend."** Then two event cards arrive one by one beneath it (target ~8.44s and ~10.54s):
- Card 1 — poster thumbnail, **"Jazz night"**, `Fri 20:30 - Kino Siska - 10 EUR - ~12 min drive`, teal `I'm going` pill.
- Card 2 — **"Farmers market"**, `Sat 08:00 - Main square - free`, teal `I'm going` pill.
Each card holds fully readable after arrival; both are on screen together for the last ~1.4s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — two cards reveal one by one, beat-aligned ~2.1s apart, each fully settled before the next.
Audio intent: quiet satisfaction — the product delivering without shouting.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft card-arrival accent per card, on the strong cues.
Music: steady bed.
Transition mood: clean to Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The move — 4.5s
A finger taps `I'm going` on the jazz card (~12.65s). The pill flips to **`Going`** with a check, in mint `#DFEDE8`. Immediate cut to the week view: Friday evening already holds a green flexible block, **"Strength training"**. The block slides cleanly from Friday to Saturday, and the teal **"Jazz night"** block drops into the opened Friday slot. One short line sits beneath: **"It moves what can move."**
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated tap, then a single decisive block movement. One motion, not a montage.
Audio intent: the one moment of quiet weight in the video — this is the differentiator.
Audio-coupled idea: confirming tap on the pill; a soft settle as the block lands.
Music: bed holds; no swell — the motion carries it.
Transition mood: soft to Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3.5s
Cards and grid dissolve to clean warm paper. The ALINE mark (teal to periwinkle infinity loops) settles centre-frame with the wordmark beneath. One line holds under it: **"Your agent makes it happen."** The video ends on paper, not on a cut to black.
Sequential/interaction: none — logo settle and one line.
Audio intent: resolve and exhale; fade out over the final ~1.5s.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle audio-reactive glow on the mark only.
Music: fade to silence.
Transition mood: end

**Music mood for this video:** warm, calm, forward — confident rather than hype
**Audio summary:** A low warm bed enters under a near-silent hook, lifts once when the product appears, marks the two card arrivals and the single tap with quiet motion-matched accents, then fades out under the logo — never louder than the idea.
